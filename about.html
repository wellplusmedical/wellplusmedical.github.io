About WellPlus Medical

WellPlus Medical is a family medicine practice caring for patients of all ages, from children through seniors. Our physicians see patients at three Tampa Bay offices in Zephyrhills, Lakeland and Lutz, Florida.

What to Expect

  • Walk-ins welcome — no appointment required, though scheduling ahead is recommended.
  • Same-day visits when you need to be seen quickly.
  • 24/7 on-call support for all three offices, so help is available after hours.
  • Telehealth — video and phone visits for follow-ups, results reviews, medication refills and many minor concerns. An in-person visit may be needed for exams or tests. Coverage varies by plan — call us to check.
